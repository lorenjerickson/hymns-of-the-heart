I have decided to embark on an ambitious, and probably unwise, undertaking. Many years ago in a Sunday School class, a teacher whom I greatly respected referred to the book of Psalms as the "whinings of a sinner." 

I was immediately offended by what I thought was an unfair characterization of David. Yes, he sinned. Yes, he was, to some extent, probably trying to make up for that through these poems about his love of the Savior. 

Until now, I have not understood how to cope with this teacher's version of the book of Psalms and the the feelings I have when I read them myself. I've decided to test my own impression of this book of scripture through music. 

Over the next several years, I intend to set each of the chapters of the book of Psalms, yes all 150 of them, to music. I depend heavily on the Spirit when writing music, so I figure if I can capture the message of these chaptered in inspired (if crude) music, then I will have refuted this teachers opinion of this book, at least in my own mind and heart. 

These compositions will focus on capturing the message of each chapter, and will not be word-for-word from the holy book. So, without further ado, what beter place to start than at the beginning. 

This is Psalms 1, from the Old Testament of the King James version of the Holy Bible.
